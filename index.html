A little clarity

Before you make the switch.

Can solar power my air conditioner?

It can, when the system is sized for the AC's electrical demand alongside your other appliances. Share the AC model, quantity and usage hours for an assessment.

What's the difference between kW and kWh?

kW describes power: how much electricity equipment uses at a moment. kWh describes energy: how much is used or stored over time. A 1 kW load running for 3 hours uses 3 kWh before system losses.

Can I upgrade my current inverter setup?

Potentially. Battery voltage, inverter specifications, charging limits and the condition of existing equipment need to be checked before recommending an upgrade.

How much will my system cost?

A quotation depends on your load, backup duration, installation requirements and available components. Prepare an enquiry above to outline your needs.
